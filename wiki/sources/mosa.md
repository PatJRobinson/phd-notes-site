# MOSA

## What it is

MOSA, Modular Open Systems Approach, is a systems engineering and procurement paradigm emphasising modular decomposition, well-defined interfaces, component interchangeability, and vendor independence.

## What it anchors in the wiki

- [Modularity](../concepts/modularity.md)
- [System boundary](../concepts/system-boundary.md)
- [Continuous assurance](../assurance/continuous-assurance.md)
- [Deployment context](../concepts/deployment-context.md)

## What it contributes

MOSA helps explain modularity and recomposition as institutional and procurement goals, as well as technical design choices. It is useful for thinking about change, heterogeneity, and ongoing integration work.

## Limits / do not overuse for

MOSA is not primarily a safety or security framework. Use it to anchor modularity and change, not to prove security or assurance outcomes by itself.
