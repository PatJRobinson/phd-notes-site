# RQ1 Evidence Map

## Evidence clusters

### Conceptual / wiki evidence

The wiki concept layer defines working understanding, partial observability, debugging, local knowledge, integrator, and system behaviour.

### Empirical design evidence

Interview guides and coding schemes operationalise RQ1 through probes about debugging, visibility, system boundaries, handoffs, deployment, and retrospective warnings.

### Methodological evidence

The methodological notes justify structured but reflexive qualitative analysis of assumptions and practices.

### Rotifer evidence

Rotifer traces are not direct practitioner evidence, but they demonstrate what artefact-mediated understanding can look like when assumptions, probes, and evidence are made explicit.

## Strong evidence

- Current RQ wording and wiki pathways.
- Interview prompt/coding scaffolding.
- Methodological notes on structured reflexive analysis.

## Weak or suggestive evidence

- Rotifer devlogs as analogues for integration understanding.
- Historical manifestos and positioning notes as project motivation.

## Missing evidence

- Actual interview/survey findings.
- Observational evidence of integrators debugging or maintaining systems.
- Examples of local knowledge transfer or loss.

## Candidate thesis material

- Working understanding vs mental model.
- Debugging as a site where understanding becomes observable.
- Local knowledge as both resource and assurance fragility.
