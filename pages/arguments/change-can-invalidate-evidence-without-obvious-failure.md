# Change Can Invalidate Evidence Without Obvious Failure

## Claim

Composition, configuration, deployment, or operational changes can weaken the meaning of previous evidence even when the system continues to run.

## Why it matters

It explains why assurance maintenance is more than checking whether the robot still works.
