# Local Knowledge

## What it is

Situated knowledge about how a particular robotic system, deployment, team, toolchain, or operating environment actually works.

## Why it matters

Local knowledge can make systems workable while remaining difficult to transfer, audit, or preserve.

## In this PhD

It is both evidence of integration practice and a potential fragility for assurance maintenance.

- RQ1: part of working understanding.
