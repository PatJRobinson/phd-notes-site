# Integrator Responsibility Boundary

## What it is

The practical and organisational boundary around who is expected to manage safety/security-relevant integration work.

## Why it matters

Security mechanisms can assume ownership and coordination capacity that may not exist in practice.

## In this PhD

Responsibility boundaries connect technical architecture to practitioner work.

## In RQ3

They explain how safety/security concerns may be handed off, dropped, contested, or made actionable.
