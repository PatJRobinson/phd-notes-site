# RQ1 Argument Map

## Core argument

Integrators construct working understanding through mediated artefacts and situated practices, not through complete access to a stable system representation.

## Supporting arguments

- Distributed robotic behaviour is visible through partial evidence surfaces.
- Debugging is an empirical window into how integrators build and revise understanding.
- Local knowledge is not inferior to formal knowledge; it is often what makes integration work.
- Understanding is maintained through routines, traces, tools, conversations, and remembered failure modes.

## Counterarguments / counterpressure

- "Mental model" language may pull the project toward inaccessible internal cognition.
- Working understanding may become too broad unless tied to observable artefacts and practices.
- Interview data will need to distinguish what practitioners report from what they actually do.

## Dependencies between arguments

- `Working understanding is artefact-mediated` `depends_on` partial observability.
- `Local knowledge is evidence and fragility` `supports` RQ4 assurance-over-change.
- `Debugging reveals system understanding` `supports` RQ3 visibility/actionability.

## Claims needing evidence

- Which artefacts integrators actually trust first.
- How teams preserve or lose local knowledge.
- What kinds of behaviour are hardest to observe.
