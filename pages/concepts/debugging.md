# Debugging

## What it is

Practical investigation, interpretation, and modification of a system when behaviour is unexpected, unclear, or unacceptable.

## Why it matters

Debugging exposes what is observable, which representations are trusted, and which controls are bypassed under pressure.

## In this PhD

Debugging is an empirical window into working understanding and safety/security actionability.

## In relevant RQs

- RQ1: how understanding is constructed.
- RQ3: how safety/security controls interact with diagnosis pressure.
