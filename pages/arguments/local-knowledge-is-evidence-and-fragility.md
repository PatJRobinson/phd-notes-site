# Local Knowledge Is Evidence and Fragility

## Claim

Local knowledge can make integration work possible while also becoming fragile when teams change, systems are redeployed, or assurance claims are revisited.

## Why it matters

This avoids treating tacit/local knowledge as simply bad, while still making its assurance implications visible.

## Supports

RQ1 and RQ4.

## Depends on

Deployment context, working understanding, continuous assurance.

## Evidence
