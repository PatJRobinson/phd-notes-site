# Safety/Security Boundaries and Nonclaims

## Boundaries

- Safety/security is a major concern area, not the only project object.
- RQ3 is the main safety/security question.
- RQ4 handles assurance claims about safety/security and other properties.
- Rotifer can evidence bounded assumptions; it does not enforce security.

## Nonclaims

- The thesis does not currently prove that ROS 2 is unsafe or insecure.
- The thesis does not claim security is always prerequisite to safety.
- The thesis does not claim SROS2 is useless.
- The thesis does not claim Rotifer replaces SROS2 or DDS Security.
- The thesis does not yet have empirical findings about practitioner behaviour.
