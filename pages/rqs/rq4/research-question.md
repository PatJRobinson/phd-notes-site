# RQ4 - Current Interpretation

## Best current wording

How do changes in system composition, configuration, and operational context affect the validity and maintenance of assurance claims for modular robotics systems?

## Plain-language version

When a robotic system changes, what happens to the claims and evidence that previously said it was safe, secure, reliable, or acceptable?

## What this question is trying to find out

It asks how assurance claims depend on boundaries, assumptions, evidence, configuration, deployment context, and local knowledge, and how those dependencies are maintained or lost over time.

## What would count as an answer

An answer would show how claims are scoped, what conditions make them valid, what kinds of change undermine them, and how evidence can be revisited or refreshed.

## What this question is not asking

It is not claiming continuous assurance is already solved. It is not asking only about formal safety cases. It is not identical to RQ3: RQ3 concerns safety/security across interfaces; RQ4 concerns claim validity and maintenance over change.

## Relation to other RQs

- RQ1 `supports` RQ4 through working understanding and local knowledge.
- RQ2 `supports` RQ4 through boundaries, modularity, and deployment context.
- RQ3 `supports` RQ4 where safety/security concerns become assurance claims.

## Current confidence

Medium-high. The wording is explicit in the current RQ redraft and wiki pathways, and the previous RQ3 compile correctly separated it as adjacent/RQ4. A new decision note keeps all four RQs for now, while preserving the tension that RQ4 must justify what change-over-time adds beyond RQ3.
