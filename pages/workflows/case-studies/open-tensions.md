# Case Studies and Articles Open Tensions

## Live tensions

- A case can easily become illustrative rather than analytical. Each case needs an explicit research connection.
- Scenario is useful as a bridge term, but it may be too broad until standards and engineering artefacts are compared closely.
- Public articles need stable argument and source grounding; research notes can remain exploratory.
- Rotifer cases risk making the constructive prototype look like the only empirical site.
- Contrast cases need public evidence before they can carry strong claims about deployment practice.

## Decisions needed

- Which candidate should become the first public article.
- Which cases belong under [06_outputs](../../../06_outputs/) as authored outputs.
- Whether scenario remains one term or splits into scenario, configuration, operating context, test case, and assurance context.
- How to mark the difference between example, contrast case, evidence source, and public explanation.

## Risks if unresolved

- The site may accumulate interesting examples without showing how they support the research questions.
- The project may overclaim from under-evidenced cases.
- Article production may drift away from the thesis argument.
