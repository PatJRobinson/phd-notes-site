# Leveson — Engineering a Safer World / STAMP

## What this is

Engineering-facing systems safety anchor. The reports describe Leveson as reframing safety as a control problem involving constraints, observability, process models, and communication across levels.

## Why it matters

Leveson gives the project a vocabulary for treating safety as an emergent property of interactions in a whole sociotechnical system, rather than as a property of isolated components.

## Role in this PhD

Use to define assumptions as underrepresented or unenforced constraints on safe and viable behaviour. Important for RQ3/RQ4 and for making the thesis legible to technical audiences.
