# Working Understanding Is Artefact-Mediated

## Claim

Integrators construct working understanding through artefacts such as logs, traces, launch files, ROS graph views, tests, documentation, simulator runs, and conversations.

## Why it matters

This gives RQ1 an observable object of study.

## Supports

RQ1, interviews-and-methodology workflow.

## Depends on

Partial observability, debugging, local knowledge.

## Evidence

Wiki concept layer and interview/methodology notes.

## Counterpressure

Avoid treating artefacts as complete records of practice.
