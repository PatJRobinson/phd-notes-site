# Modularity Shifts Work to Integration

## Claim

Modularity enables reuse and flexibility but shifts system-level behaviour, boundary, and assurance work to integration.

## Why it matters

This is the mechanism linking ROS 2-style composition to RQ2 and RQ4.

## Supports

RQ2, RQ4, thesis-core workflow.

## Depends on

Integration, system boundary, deployment context.

## Evidence

Modularity concept page, fragmentation note, Rotifer provider/realisations work.

## Counterpressure

Modularity itself is not the problem; the issue is untracked assumptions across composition.
