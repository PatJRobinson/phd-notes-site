# Partial Observability Shapes Integration Practice

## Claim

What integrators can understand, manage, or assure depends on which system states are visible and interpretable.

## Why it matters

This links RQ1 working understanding to RQ3 safety/security actionability and RQ4 assurance maintenance.

## Supports

RQ1, RQ3, RQ4.

## Depends on

Visibility, debugging, evidence surfaces.

## Evidence

Core interview concepts, wiki concept layer, Rotifer evidence ladder.

## Counterpressure

Partial observability should not become a vague claim that systems are unknowable.
