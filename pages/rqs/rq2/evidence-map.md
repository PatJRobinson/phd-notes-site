# RQ2 Evidence Map

## Evidence clusters

### Conceptual boundary layer

Wiki pages define system boundary, modularity, deployment context, configuration, and interface.

### Historical/theoretical layer

Fragmentation and architectural instability notes provide the strongest developed argument that boundaries become unstable or ambiguous under modularity and heterogeneous integration.

### Technical architecture layer

DDS/ROS2 notes and source anchors support concrete boundary analysis around domains, participants, topics, QoS, SROS2, and ROS graph abstractions.

### Rotifer layer

Provider, realisation, deployment trace, and environment manifest devlogs show a prototype vocabulary for separating what is intended, realised, deployed, and evidenced.

## Strong evidence

- RQ and wiki concept layer.
- Fragmentation/architectural instability note.
- Rotifer provider/deployment devlogs.

## Weak or suggestive evidence

- ROSness note is useful but contains web-derived claims that need source audit.
- MOSA/autonomy role material is promising but not yet fully integrated.

## Missing evidence

- Empirical examples of practitioners drawing different boundaries.
- Systematic classification of boundary types.
- Literature-backed MOSA integration with RQ2.

## Candidate thesis material

- "A ROS graph is not the system boundary."
- Provider substitution as a boundary test.
- Deployment context as a first-class condition of claims.
