# SACE Safety Assurance for Autonomous Systems

## What this is

Safety-assurance guidance associated in the reports with the University of York ecosystem. The progression report uses it as support for the claim that justified confidence in safety is difficult in complex and changing real-world environments.

## Why it matters

It strengthens the RQ4 assurance-over-change thread and helps connect the thesis to contemporary autonomous-systems assurance work.

## Role in this PhD

Use for RQ4, especially when arguing that assurance claims need scope, evidence, context, and maintenance over change.
