# Safety/Security Relation to RQ3

RQ3 is the primary home for safety/security:

> How do safety and security concerns emerge and get managed across the interfaces between components, tools, frameworks, and engineering practices in robotic system integration?

## RQ3 role

RQ3 turns safety/security from a generic motivation into a specific research question about interfaces, management, responsibility, and visibility.

## RQ3 guardrails

- It does not assume security is always the cause of safety problems.
- It does not collapse safety/security/assurance.
- It asks how concerns meet, blur, separate, or become actionable.
