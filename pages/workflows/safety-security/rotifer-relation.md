# Safety/Security Relation to Rotifer

## Current relation

Rotifer is relevant to safety/security when it represents and checks safety/security-relevant assumptions such as command authority.

## What Rotifer contributes

- Declares an assumption.
- Runs baseline and violation conditions.
- Collects graph-level and behaviour-level evidence.
- Marks a violated run as invalid for the original claim.

## What Rotifer does not contribute yet

- Enforcement.
- Authentication.
- Complete threat modelling.
- Production assurance.
- General safety validation.
