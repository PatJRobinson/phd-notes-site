# Portugal et al. — ROS 2 Adoption

## What this is

Current ROS 2 adoption/practice anchor. The search appendix report describes it as a user-experience questionnaire study with more than 100 participants, examining adoption levels, migration blockers, and expectations.

## Why it matters

Hidden assumptions often become visible during migration, integration, and operational use. Adoption evidence helps keep the thesis connected to actual ROS 2 practice rather than only specifications.

## Role in this PhD

Use for RQ1/RQ2 support around practitioner expectations, migration barriers, and where ROS 2 does or does not meet user needs.
