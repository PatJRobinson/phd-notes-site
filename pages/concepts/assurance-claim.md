# Assurance Claim

## What it is

A justified statement that a system has, maintains, or satisfies some property under specified conditions.

## Why it matters

Assurance is about claim, evidence, reasoning, scope, validity, and maintenance, not the property alone.

## In this PhD

Assurance claim vocabulary keeps safety/security distinct from the evidence and reasoning used to justify them.
