# IEC 62443-3-2

## What this is

Industrial automation and control systems security standard anchor. The reports highlight system-under-consideration, zones, conduits, and risk assessment.

## Why it matters

It embodies a normative assumption that system boundaries, zones, conduits, and responsibilities can be sufficiently defined to support risk and assurance work.

## Role in this PhD

Use in RQ2/RQ3/RQ4 when discussing system boundaries, segmentation, security risk assessment, and whether modular robotics systems fit existing industrial-security abstractions.
