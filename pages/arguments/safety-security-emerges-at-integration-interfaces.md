# Safety/Security Emerges at Integration Interfaces

## Claim

In ROS 2-style robotics, safety/security concerns often emerge at interfaces between components, tools, frameworks, deployment contexts, and engineering practices rather than inside a single isolated component.

## Why it matters

This is the core RQ3 argument. It makes integration a safety/security research site.

## Supports

RQ3 current interpretation.

## Depends on

Interface, integration, configuration, safety/security relation.

## Evidence

Current RQ wording, wiki concept layer, interview concept map, and Rotifer/Nav2 evidence ladder.

## Counterpressure
