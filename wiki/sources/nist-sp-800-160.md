# NIST SP 800-160

## What it is

NIST SP 800-160 is a systems security engineering standard concerned with trustworthy systems, lifecycle reasoning, system boundaries, requirements, architecture, and assurance.

## What it anchors in the wiki

- [Assurance claim](../assurance/assurance-claim.md)
- [Continuous assurance](../assurance/continuous-assurance.md)
- [System boundary](../concepts/system-boundary.md)
- [Safety/security relation](../assurance/safety-security-relation.md)

## What it contributes

It anchors a formal systems-engineering view of security as a system property tied to architecture, lifecycle, responsibility, and evidence. It is useful as a contrast to the more situated and changing conditions of ROS 2-style integration.

## Limits / do not overuse for

Do not use it as direct evidence of robotics practice. It is strongest as a normative systems-engineering source.
