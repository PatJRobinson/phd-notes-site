# Integration

## What it is

Integration is the work of making components, configurations, infrastructure, tools, documentation, and operating contexts function together as a robotic system.

## Why it matters

Integration is where system behaviour becomes more than component behaviour.

## In this PhD

The thesis treats integration as a central site where behaviour, boundaries, and safety/security concerns are constructed.

## In RQ3

RQ3 studies safety/security at integration interfaces.
