# System Behaviour

## What it is

What the robotic system does when components, configurations, middleware, infrastructure, and operating context interact.

## Why it matters

Behaviour may be distributed across layers and cannot always be inferred from component code alone.

## In this PhD

System behaviour is partially constructed through integration and interpreted through evidence surfaces.

## In relevant RQs

- RQ1: what integrators construct understanding of.
- RQ2: what boundaries are drawn around.
