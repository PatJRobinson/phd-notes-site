# Star and Ruhleder — Infrastructure

## What this is

Infrastructure studies anchor. The reports use Star and Ruhleder to theorise invisible infrastructure, installed base, standards, breakdown, and invisible work.

## Why it matters

This is central to the thesis because hidden assumptions are often infrastructural conditions that become visible through breakdowns, workarounds, and cross-boundary maintenance practices.

## Role in this PhD

Use for RQ1/RQ2/RQ3 to explain why assumptions are hard to see in advance and why they emerge at boundaries between tools, standards, contexts, and work practices.
