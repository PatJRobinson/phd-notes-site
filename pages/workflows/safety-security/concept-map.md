# Safety/Security Concept Map

## Safety/security relation

Distinct concerns that can become entangled in robotics because cyber behaviour can have physical consequences.

## Interface

Where concerns often emerge: topics, DDS entities, configuration, deployment, documentation, tools, and responsibility handoffs.

## Command authority

Current strongest safety/security worked example.

## Configuration

Where policies, permissions, QoS, launch choices, and runtime assumptions shape safety/security.

## Responsibility boundary

Where safety/security work may be assumed, handed off, or dropped.

## Assurance claim

Where safety/security properties become claims requiring evidence and maintenance.
