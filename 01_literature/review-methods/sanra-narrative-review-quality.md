# SANRA Narrative Review Quality

## What this is

SANRA is used in the reports as a quality anchor for non-systematic narrative reviews, especially because it treats transparent description of the literature search as a quality criterion.

## Why it matters

This helps defend a non-SLR review without making it look casual. It supports the claim that a narrative or integrative review can be methodologically credible when search logic, scope, sources, and selection rules are explicit.

## Role in this PhD

Use SANRA to justify the search appendix and the review-method paragraph in the progression report.
