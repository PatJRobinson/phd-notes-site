## Relations between work areas

- Thesis core `depends_on` the organised RQ notes.
- Rotifer paper `supports` RQ2, RQ3, and RQ4 as executable evidence, while also offering RQ1-relevant artefact-mediated understanding.
- Safety/security `extends` RQ3 and informs RQ4, but should not overtake the whole thesis.
- Interviews and methodology `supports` RQ1 and gives empirical routes into RQ3/RQ4.
- Case studies and articles `supports` RQ2/RQ3/RQ4 by grounding abstract claims in concrete projects, deployments, standards, and explanatory outputs.
- Recoverable fragments preserve older framings without making them current claims.
