# Working Understanding

## Short definition

Working understanding is a situated, practical, and partial grasp of how a system behaves well enough to build, operate, debug, or justify it.

## Why it matters

In distributed robotics, understanding is mediated by artefacts: logs, launch files, traces, diagrams, package conventions, tests, simulator runs, documentation, and conversations with other practitioners.

Working understanding matters because integrators often need to act under uncertainty. They may need to decide whether a system is behaving normally, whether a failure is local or systemic, whether a configuration change is safe, or whether evidence still supports an assurance claim.

## In this PhD

The current RQ framing asks how integrators construct and maintain working understandings of system behaviour. This page treats working understanding as practical and evidence-mediated, not as direct access to internal mental models.
