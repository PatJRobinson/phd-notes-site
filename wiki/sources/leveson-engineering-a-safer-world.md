# Leveson: Engineering a Safer World

## What it is

Leveson’s systems-theoretic account of safety, including STAMP/STPA, emergent system properties, constraints, control, observability, and process models.

## What it anchors in the wiki

- [Safety/security relation](../assurance/safety-security-relation.md)
- [Assurance claim](../assurance/assurance-claim.md)
- [System behaviour](../concepts/system-behaviour.md)
- [Continuous assurance](../assurance/continuous-assurance.md)

## What it contributes

It helps frame safety as a system property involving constraints, control, feedback, and socio-technical interaction. This is useful for pages that avoid reducing safety to component correctness.

## Limits / do not overuse for

Do not make Leveson carry the entire thesis theory by default. Use it where system safety, control, constraints, and observability are specifically relevant.
