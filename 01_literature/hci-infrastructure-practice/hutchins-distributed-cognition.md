# Hutchins — Distributed Cognition

## What this is

Distributed cognition anchor. The reports describe Hutchins as shifting the unit of analysis from an isolated individual mind to a sociotechnical cognitive system distributed across people, artefacts, representations, environments, and time.

## Why it matters

This supports the RQ1 focus on how integrators construct working understanding through tools, traces, logs, conversations, documentation, and local experience.

## Role in this PhD

Use to avoid reducing the empirical study to individual mental models. The relevant unit is the coordination system around robotics integration work.
