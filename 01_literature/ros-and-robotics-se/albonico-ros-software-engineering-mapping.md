# Albonico et al. — ROS Software Engineering Mapping Study

## What this is

Seed anchor for software-engineering research on ROS. The reports describe it as a systematic field-level baseline with a classification framework and explicit industrial relevance.

## Why it matters

It shows that ROS software engineering is a recognised research terrain rather than merely a convenient case setting.

## Role in this PhD

Use to position the thesis as more focused and more cross-layer than the general robotics-SE literature. It supports RQ2 by grounding ROS as a software-engineering and integration field.
