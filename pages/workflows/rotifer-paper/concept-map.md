# Rotifer Paper Concept Map

## Experiment claim

The claim a run is meant to support. In command authority, the claim is that robot motion is attributable only to declared command-authority roles.

## Assumption

A condition that must hold for the claim to remain meaningful.

## Provider / realisation

The concrete execution substrate and binding through which scenario intent becomes runnable.

## Deployment trace

The resolved account of where roles run and which deployment assumptions are made visible.

## Probe and evidence

The mechanism for inspecting runtime conditions and binding observations to assumption outcomes.

## Command authority

The flagship safety/security-relevant assumption candidate.
