# RQ3 Evidence Map

## Evidence clusters

### Current RQ and concept layer

The RQ redraft, wiki RQ pathways, and wiki concept pages provide the strongest evidence for current framing and concept boundaries. They are not empirical evidence of RQ3 phenomena, but they are strong provenance for the current interpretation.

### Interview / empirical design concepts

The interview concept map identifies responsibility boundaries, trust in abstractions, operational pressure, visibility, lifecycle drift, and cross-boundary coordination as concepts to investigate with developers. This is strong design evidence and candidate empirical coding structure, but not yet findings.

### Rotifer and Nav2 executable evidence

Rotifer notes and the Nav2 evidence ladder show how cross-layer assumptions can be declared, probed, and classified. This is strong material evidence for the feasibility of assumption tracing and for the claim that topic presence is weaker than semantic frame/timing compatibility. It is weaker evidence for broad practitioner management of safety/security unless connected to interviews or case analysis.

### DDS/ROS2/SROS2 architectural substrate

DDS/ROS2 notes identify where invariants, security boundaries, discovery, QoS, and permissions attach. This supports the technical side of RQ3 but should be anchored in literature/source notes before being treated as thesis evidence.

### Historical layer-misalignment and fragmentation arguments

These notes provide strong conceptual precursors for RQ3. They should be used carefully because some language is mechanism-first and may overstate the current neutral RQ framing.

## Missing evidence

- Direct interview/survey evidence about how practitioners manage RQ3 concerns.
- Specific SROS2/DDS security policy case studies tied to actual integration work.
- Clear examples where a security concern creates, changes, or is separated from a safety concern.
- Evidence that responsibility handoffs are observed rather than only hypothesised.

## Candidate thesis material

- Command authority as a worked RQ3 case.
- Visibility/actionability as bridge between interviews and Rotifer probes.
- Operational pressure as a management mechanism.
- "Topic presence is weaker than semantic compatibility" as a reusable evidence pattern.
- Safety/security relation as a boundary-setting section.
