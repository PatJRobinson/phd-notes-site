# Hollnagel: Work-as-Imagined / Work-as-Done

## What it is

Hollnagel’s resilience engineering work is used here for the distinction between Work-as-Imagined and Work-as-Done, and for attention to adaptive practice in complex socio-technical systems.

## What it anchors in the wiki

- [Working understanding](../concepts/working-understanding.md)
- [Debugging](../practice/debugging.md)
- [Local knowledge](../practice/local-knowledge.md)
- [Integrator](../practice/integrator.md)

## What it contributes

It helps explain why formal descriptions, procedures, and designs may differ from situated practice. It supports careful attention to how integrators adapt, coordinate, and keep systems working.

## Limits / do not overuse for

Do not use it to imply that formal artefacts are unimportant. The wiki should use it to explain the relationship between documented systems and practice, not to dismiss documentation or engineering structure.
