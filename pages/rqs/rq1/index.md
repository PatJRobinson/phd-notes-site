# RQ1 Compilation

## Current wording or inferred wording

Best current wording: How do integrators construct and maintain working understandings of behaviour in distributed robotics systems?

This is explicit in [wiki/meta/rq-pathways.md](../../../wiki/meta/rq-pathways.md) and appears first in [research-questions-redraft-16-06-26.md](../../../03_research_questions/research-questions-redraft-16-06-26.md).

## What this question is really asking

RQ1 asks how practitioners know enough to act in systems whose behaviour is distributed across ROS graphs, launch files, logs, middleware, configuration, deployment context, local conventions, and other people. It is not trying to read minds directly; it treats understanding as situated, practical, partial, and visible through work.

## Why it matters

The thesis needs an account of how system understanding is produced before it can explain safety/security management or assurance maintenance. If integrators only see the system through partial artefacts, then evidence, responsibility, and claims will also be partial.

## How it relates to the thesis

RQ1 provides the practice/epistemic foundation for the other RQs. RQ2 asks what system boundaries become under modularity; RQ3 asks how safety/security concerns emerge across interfaces; RQ4 asks how assurance claims survive change. RQ1 explains the human and artefact-mediated work that makes those questions researchable.
