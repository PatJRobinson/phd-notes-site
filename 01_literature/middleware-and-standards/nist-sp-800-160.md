# NIST SP 800-160

## What this is

Systems security engineering anchor. The reports describe it as framing trustworthy systems engineering in terms of complexity, dynamicity, interconnectedness, and machine/physical/human components.

## Why it matters

It supports the thesis claim that security and assurance are systems-engineering problems across complex cyber-physical and system-of-systems contexts.

## Role in this PhD

Use for RQ3 and RQ4, especially where the thesis needs a standards-backed account of trustworthy systems, assurance, and system context.
