# Safety/Security Relation

## Short definition

Safety and security are distinct concerns that can become entangled in cyber-physical robotic systems.

## Why it matters

Safety concerns harm, hazards, operational consequences, and unacceptable risk. Security concerns adversarial or unauthorised influence, exposure, misuse, compromise, and control over system behaviour or data. In robotics, security failures can have safety consequences because software and communication affect physical action.

The useful rhetorical idea is that in cyber-physical systems there may be no safety without security. This should not be read as collapsing the two concepts completely. They involve different traditions, evidence practices, standards, and responsibilities.

## In this PhD

This page keeps safety/security distinct from assurance. The project is interested in how safety and security concerns emerge across interfaces during integration, and how claims about those concerns are justified and maintained.
