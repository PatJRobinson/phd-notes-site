# Integrator

## What it is

A functional role for anyone making components, tools, configurations, infrastructure, and operating contexts function together as a robotic system.

## Why it matters

System-level responsibility often lands on integration work, even when the formal job title is developer, operator, deployment engineer, or researcher.

## In this PhD

Integrator is the main practitioner figure linking architecture, work practice, responsibility, and evidence.

## In relevant RQs

- RQ1: integrators construct and maintain working understanding.
- RQ3: integrators manage or fail to manage safety/security concerns across interfaces.
- RQ4: integrators may maintain the local knowledge that keeps claims meaningful.
