# ISO 10218

## What it is

ISO 10218 is an industrial robot safety standard. In this wiki it is treated as a safety source relevant to robot hazards, operational safety, and safety-oriented constraints.

## What it anchors in the wiki

- [Safety/security relation](../assurance/safety-security-relation.md)
- [Assurance claim](../assurance/assurance-claim.md)
- [System behaviour](../concepts/system-behaviour.md)

## What it contributes

It helps anchor the safety side of safety/security discussions, especially where security-relevant control or communication issues may affect physical robot behaviour.

## Limits / do not overuse for

Do not use ISO 10218 as a general robotics security source. It is a safety anchor, and its application outside industrial robot contexts needs author review.
