# RQ4 Evidence Map

## Evidence clusters

### Assurance concept layer

Wiki assurance pages define assurance claims and continuous assurance cautiously.

### Fragmentation and instability layer

The fragmentation note argues that modular, heterogeneous, changing systems complicate boundary definition, component reasoning, property verification, and responsibility.

### Rotifer evidence layer

Rotifer positioning, project ideology, and evidence-ladder notes show how assumptions can be tied to evidence and revisited across realisations and contexts.

### Interview/methodology layer

Interview concepts around lifecycle drift, visibility, local knowledge, and deployment/change over time provide empirical routes into RQ4.

## Strong evidence

- Assurance wiki pages for concept definitions.
- Fragmentation note for motivation.
- Rotifer positioning note for assumption/evidence tracing.

## Weak or suggestive evidence

- Architecture instability / human role note as broader contextualisation.
- NotebookLM/commissioned fragments, if used later, require human judgement and source audit.

## Missing evidence

- Actual assurance case examples.
- Practitioner accounts of evidence becoming stale.
- Standards-specific analysis of claim maintenance.
- Evaluation of Rotifer as an assurance-support research method.

- Rotifer as evidence provenance for bounded claims.
