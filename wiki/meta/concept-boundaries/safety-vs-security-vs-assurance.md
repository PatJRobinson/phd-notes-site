# Safety vs Security vs Assurance

## Distinction

Safety concerns hazards, harm, operational consequences, and unacceptable risk. Security concerns unauthorised or adversarial influence, exposure, misuse, compromise, and control. Assurance concerns claims, evidence, justification, validity, scope, and maintenance.

## Why the distinction matters

Robotics and cyber-physical systems can link security failures to safety consequences. That link does not make assurance the same kind of concept. Assurance is the work of justifying and maintaining confidence in safety/security-related claims.

## Common confusion

Safety and security are often discussed together because both affect system risk. Assurance is often pulled into the same discussion because safety/security claims need evidence. The terms should remain analytically separate.

## Usage in this wiki

Use safety/security for system concerns and consequences. Use assurance for the claims and evidence about those concerns.
