# Rotifer Paper Positioning

## What Rotifer is doing intellectually

Rotifer makes integration work legible as a process of declaring, varying, probing, and interpreting assumptions.

## What Rotifer is doing technically

It represents apps, experiments, providers, realisations, contracts, deployment targets, probes, evidence, and assumption outcomes.

## Current boundary

The paper should be framed around assumption tracing and evidence interpretation. The current source-layer decision is that the Nav2 ladder carries this methodological argument. Safety/security should appear as motivation and consequence rather than as the demonstrated centre.

## Strong positioning sentence

Rotifer changes the epistemic status of a robotics experiment run: from an opaque success/failure event into an inspectable account of what had to hold for the claim to mean what it says.

## Safety/security positioning

Safety/security belongs in the paper as an implication of explicit integration knowledge: safety and security arguments require knowing what system was realised, what assumptions were active, and what evidence supports behavioural claims. This should not become a claim that the paper evaluates safety or security properties.

## Developer-facing design positioning

A newer source note frames Rotifer as a possible vehicle for design-oriented research into developer-facing tools for complex robotics integration. In that framing, the design question is not visual polish or convenience in isolation. It is how a tool represents system structure, exposes relationships, communicates uncertainty, and supports appropriate developer understanding and action.

This direction fits the existing Rotifer argument if it remains tied to assumptions, interfaces, evidence, system change, and working understanding. It should not yet be presented as a settled design PhD contribution or as an evaluated tool-design result.
