# Working Understanding vs Mental Model

## Distinction

Working understanding is observable through situated practice, artefacts, explanations, debugging, and coordination. A mental model refers to a person’s internal representation of how something works.

## Why the distinction matters

The wiki should avoid claiming direct access to internal mental models unless the evidence supports that. Working understanding gives the project a way to study understanding through visible practice and artefacts.

## Common confusion

Interview answers, diagrams, explanations, and debugging practices can reveal something about mental models, but they are not identical to the internal model itself.

## Usage in this wiki

Use working understanding for practical, artefact-mediated understanding. Use mental model only when the source or method specifically supports that framing.
