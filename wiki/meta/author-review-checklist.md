# Author Review Checklist

Back to [wiki index](../index.md).

Use this checklist before treating the wiki as stable.

- Are definitions consistent with the current research questions?
- Did any page accidentally become a thesis argument?
- Is assumption/misalignment language over-dominant?
- Are safety/security concerns kept distinct from assurance claims?
- Is assurance described in terms of claims, evidence, validity, and maintenance?
- Is integrator defined functionally rather than by job title?
- Are source anchors real and useful?
- Are any pages too generic?
- Are any pages too confident?
- Are uncertainty markers used where needed?
- Are concepts missing, especially standards, evidence ladder, Rotifer, responsibility boundaries, observability, or lifecycle drift?
- Are old framings preserved without being treated as current premises?
- Are links useful in plain markdown?
- Does any page overuse “not just X, but Y” style phrasing?
- Does any page slip into thesis-argument tone?
- Are safety/security concerns kept separate from assurance claims?
- Is assumption/misalignment vocabulary used only where it helps?
- Are source-anchor pages linked from relevant concept pages?
- Are any source anchors too weak, generic, or uncited?
- Are concept-boundary pages accurate enough to guide future writing?
- Are any pages still too generic to be useful?
