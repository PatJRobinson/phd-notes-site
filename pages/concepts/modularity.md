# Modularity

## What it is

Organisation into separable components that can be developed, configured, replaced, composed, or reused.

## Why it matters

Modularity supports flexibility and reuse while shifting system-level work to integration.

## In this PhD

Modularity is a condition under which boundaries, behaviours, and assurance claims become variable.

## In relevant RQs

- RQ2: shapes system boundaries.
- RQ4: produces change that affects assurance claims.
