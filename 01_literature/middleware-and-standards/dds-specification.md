# DDS Specification

## What this is

Object Management Group specification for the Data Distribution Service communication model that underlies ROS 2 middleware implementations.

## Why it matters

The reports identify DDS as part of the normative middleware layer: communication semantics, publish/subscribe structure, discovery, QoS, and interoperability.

## Role in this PhD

Use to analyse system boundaries and assumptions encoded below the ROS abstraction. Important for RQ2 and RQ3.
