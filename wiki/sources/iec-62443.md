# IEC 62443

## What it is

IEC 62443 is a family of standards for cybersecurity in industrial automation and control systems.

## What it anchors in the wiki

- [Safety/security relation](../assurance/safety-security-relation.md)
- [System boundary](../concepts/system-boundary.md)
- [Interface](../concepts/interface.md)
- [Assurance claim](../assurance/assurance-claim.md)

## What it contributes

It provides vocabulary for zones, conduits, security levels, responsibility, and lifecycle-oriented security governance. These ideas are useful when thinking about how systems become governable through decomposition and controlled interaction.

## Limits / do not overuse for

IEC 62443 is rooted in industrial automation and control-system settings. It should not be treated as a complete fit for mobile, modular, or ROS 2-style robotics without careful translation.
