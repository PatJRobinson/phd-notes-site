## What this is

Anchor for the limits of plans, formal representations, and prescriptive accounts of action.

## Why it matters

The theoretical foundation report says Suchman's critique is pertinent because plans and formal representations presuppose contingent forms of action that they cannot fully specify.

## Role in this PhD

Use to explain why architecture documents, launch files, standards, and policies do not fully determine situated integration practice.
