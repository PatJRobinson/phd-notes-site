# Rotifer Positioning and Project Ideology

## What it is

Notes positioning Rotifer as infrastructure for stating, tracing, testing, and interpreting the assumptions that make robotics experiment claims meaningful.

A newer source note adds a candidate design-research route: Rotifer may help study how developer-facing tools represent system structure, uncertainty, assumptions, evidence, and change for practical integration work.

## Why it matters

They show the prototype's intended research role: making composition and integration legible.

## In this PhD

Rotifer is executable evidence and a methodological probe into integration assumptions. It may also become a design probe for developer-facing integration tools, but that claim needs further grounding.

## In RQ3

Rotifer provides a way to study safety/security-relevant interface assumptions materially.
