# Rotifer Paper Positioning

## What Rotifer is doing intellectually

Rotifer makes integration work legible as a process of declaring, varying, probing, and interpreting assumptions.

## What Rotifer is doing technically

It represents apps, experiments, providers, realisations, contracts, deployment targets, probes, evidence, and assumption outcomes.

## Current boundary

The paper should be framed around assumption tracing and evidence interpretation, not around general portability, benchmarking, security scanning, or assurance automation. The current source-layer decision is that the Nav2 ladder carries this methodological argument. Safety/security should appear as motivation and consequence, not as the demonstrated centre.

## Strong positioning sentence

Rotifer changes the epistemic status of a robotics experiment run: from an opaque success/failure event into an inspectable account of what had to hold for the claim to mean what it says.

## Safety/security positioning

Safety/security belongs in the paper as an implication of explicit integration knowledge: safety and security arguments require knowing what system was realised, what assumptions were active, and what evidence supports behavioural claims. This should not become a claim that the paper evaluates safety or security properties.
