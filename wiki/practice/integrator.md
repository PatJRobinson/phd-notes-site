# Integrator

## Short definition

An integrator is any practitioner whose work involves making components, tools, configurations, infrastructure, and operating contexts function together as a robotic system.

## Why it matters

Integrator is a functional category, not a job title. It may include developers, roboticists, research engineers, deployment engineers, operators, field engineers, or security/operations staff when they are acting in an integration capacity.

This matters because ROS 2-style robotics often places system-level responsibility on whoever has to make the system work across layers. That person may not be the original component author, the formal safety owner, or the person who wrote the security policy.

## In this PhD

The current RQs use integrators as a key practitioner category. The emphasis is on how integration work constructs and maintains system understanding, manages safety/security concerns, and supports or weakens assurance claims.
