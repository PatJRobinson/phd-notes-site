# Progression Literature Survey Requirement

## What this is

Institutional framing note for what the progression literature survey needs to demonstrate.

## Why it matters

The progression literature survey report argues that the requirement is breadth, judgement, critical evaluation, and defensible positioning. It does not require a strict systematic literature review.

## Role in this PhD

Use this to keep the progression literature work proportionate. The review should show which literatures matter, how they relate, where they disagree, and what gap the thesis occupies.
