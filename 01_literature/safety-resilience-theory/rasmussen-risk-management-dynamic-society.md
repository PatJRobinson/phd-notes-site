# Rasmussen — Risk Management in a Dynamic Society

## What this is

Cross-level sociotechnical risk and control anchor. The reports describe Rasmussen as perhaps the most direct fit for studying assumptions across standards, middleware, tooling, and practice.

## Why it matters

Rasmussen models safety as a sociotechnical control problem spanning multiple levels, and his dynamic-society work gives a way to theorise boundary movement, pressure, and migration toward limits of acceptable performance.

## Role in this PhD

Use as a primary structural theory for RQ2/RQ4: assumptions and margins move across lifecycle stages, organisational levels, and deployment contexts.
