# Working Understanding

## What it is

A situated, practical, partial grasp of how a system behaves well enough to build, operate, debug, change, or justify it.

## Why it matters

It gives the project an operational way to discuss understanding through practice and evidence surfaces. A new decision note records that "mental models" and "working understanding" can be used more or less interchangeably at this stage; working understanding remains the safer term when making methodological claims about observable practice.

## In this PhD

Working understanding is the RQ1 core and a support concept for RQ4 assurance maintenance.

## In relevant RQs

- RQ1: central object.
- RQ4: claim maintenance depends on what remains understood when systems change.
