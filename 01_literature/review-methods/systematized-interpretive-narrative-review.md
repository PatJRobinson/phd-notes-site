# Systematized Interpretive Narrative Review

## What this is

A disciplined narrative review form: interpretive in synthesis, but explicit about search strands, source boundaries, inclusion/exclusion logic, and search logging.

## Why it matters

The search appendix report recommends this as the most defensible progression-exam position. The project is too heterogeneous for a narrow aggregative systematic review, but too important to leave as an ad hoc narrative review.

## Role in this PhD

This supports a literature review that searches by strand: ROS/ROS 2 software engineering, middleware and DDS, standards and assurance, misconfiguration and integration failure, adoption and practice, safety/resilience theory, HCI/distributed cognition, infrastructure/STS, and bounded grey literature.
