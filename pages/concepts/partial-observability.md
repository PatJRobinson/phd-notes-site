# Partial Observability

## What it is

The condition that system behaviour is visible only through limited, mediated, situated representations.

## Why it matters

What can be managed or assured depends partly on what can be observed and interpreted.

## In this PhD

Partial observability connects integrator practice, debugging, Rotifer probes, and assurance evidence.
