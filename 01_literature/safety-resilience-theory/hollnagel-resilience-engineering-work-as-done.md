## What this is

Resilience-engineering anchor for the gap between formal descriptions and practical adaptation.

## Why it matters

The reports say Hollnagel contributes the practice-facing part that Perrow and Leveson do not fully provide: why work-as-imagined and work-as-done diverge, and why studying practice is central to safety and resilience.
