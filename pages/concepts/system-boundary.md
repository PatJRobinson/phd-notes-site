# System Boundary

## What it is

The practical or analytical line around what counts as part of the system for a particular purpose.

## Why it matters

Safety/security and assurance claims need scope, but scope can vary across design, deployment, runtime, debugging, responsibility, and evidence.

## In this PhD

System boundary is treated as an empirical and analytical question, not a fixed premise.
