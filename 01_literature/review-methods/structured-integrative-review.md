# Structured Integrative Review

## What this is

Review identity for heterogeneous, cross-disciplinary material where the goal is to review, critique, and synthesise representative literature rather than aggregate a single homogeneous evidence base.

## Why it matters

The progression literature report argues that the project should not force standards, middleware documentation, empirical studies, theory, and practice accounts into a strict systematic-review form. An integrative review is a better fit because the thesis is building a layered account of hidden assumptions across different kinds of source.

## Role in this PhD

This is one of the strongest labels for the progression-stage literature review. It signals breadth, judgement, transparency, and synthesis without claiming exhaustive SLR coverage.
