# RQ1 Concept Map

## Integrator

### What it is

A functional role: anyone making components, tools, configurations, infrastructure, and operating contexts work together.

### Why it matters

System-level responsibility often lands on the person doing integration work, regardless of formal job title.

### Role in RQ1

The integrator is the primary actor whose working understanding RQ1 studies.

### Related concepts

Working understanding, debugging, local knowledge, configuration, deployment context.

### Source provenance

[wiki/practice/integrator.md](../../../wiki/practice/integrator.md), [Core Concepts.md](../../../02_my_notes/Interview%20Preperation/Core%20Concepts.md).

### Status

## Working understanding

### What it is

A situated, practical, and partial grasp of how a system behaves well enough to build, operate, debug, or justify it.

### Why it matters

It avoids both "perfect knowledge" and "ignorance" framings.

### Role in RQ1

This is the core concept.

### Related concepts

Partial observability, local knowledge, debugging, system behaviour.

### Source provenance

[wiki/concepts/working-understanding.md](../../../wiki/concepts/working-understanding.md), [note-on-analytical-approach.md](../../../04_research_methodologies/note-on-analytical-approach.md).

### Status

active

## Partial observability

### What it is

System behaviour is visible through limited, mediated, situated representations.

### Why it matters

Understanding depends on what can be observed and interpreted.

### Role in RQ1

It explains why working understanding is constructed rather than simply possessed.

### Related concepts

Visibility, debugging, ROS graph, Rotifer traces.

### Source provenance

[wiki/concepts/partial-observability.md](../../../wiki/concepts/partial-observability.md), [Canonical Nav2 Evidence Ladder.md](../../../02_my_notes/papers/Making%20Command%20Authority%20Assumptions%20Explicit%20in%20ROS%202%20Robotics%20Experiments/Canonical%20Nav2%20Evidence%20Ladder.md).

### Status

active

## Local knowledge

### What it is

Situated knowledge about a particular system, team, deployment, toolchain, or operating environment.

### Why it matters

It can make systems workable while being hard to transfer, audit, preserve, or include in assurance.

### Role in RQ1

It is one way working understanding is maintained.

### Related concepts

Integrator, deployment context, continuous assurance, debugging.

### Source provenance

[wiki/practice/local-knowledge.md](../../../wiki/practice/local-knowledge.md), [Core Concepts.md](../../../02_my_notes/Interview%20Preperation/Core%20Concepts.md).
