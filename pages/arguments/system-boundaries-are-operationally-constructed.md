# System Boundaries Are Operationally Constructed

## Claim

System boundaries in ROS 2-style robotics are practical and analytical constructions shaped by modularity, configuration, deployment, responsibility, and evidence needs.

## Why it matters

Boundary definition underlies safety/security reasoning and assurance claim scope.

## Supports

RQ2 and RQ4.

## Depends on

System boundary, deployment context, configuration, modularity.

## Evidence

Wiki concept layer and fragmentation note.

## Counterpressure

Do not overstate this as "boundaries are arbitrary" or "boundaries never stabilise."
