# Hutchins: Cognition in the Wild

## What it is

Hutchins’ work on distributed cognition treats cognition as distributed across people, artefacts, representations, and environments.

## What it anchors in the wiki

## What it contributes

It helps move the wiki away from treating understanding as a purely internal mental state. It supports the framing of understanding as distributed across tools, documentation, runtime artefacts, logs, infrastructure, and collaborators.

## Limits / do not overuse for

Do not use it as evidence for specific ROS 2 practice unless paired with empirical material. It is a conceptual source for distributed cognition and artefact-mediated understanding.
