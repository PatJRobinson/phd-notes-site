# RQ1 - Current Interpretation

## Best current wording

How do integrators construct and maintain working understandings of behaviour in distributed robotics systems?

## Plain-language version

How do people who build and integrate robotic systems figure out what the system is doing well enough to debug, operate, change, or justify it?

## What this question is trying to find out

It asks how understanding is assembled through artefacts and practice: logs, ROS graph views, topic echoes, launch files, traces, simulator runs, documentation, tests, conversations, and local experience.

## What would count as an answer

An answer would identify the main evidence surfaces integrators use, the kinds of system behaviour they can and cannot see, how local knowledge accumulates, and how understanding is maintained or lost across change.

## What this question is not asking

It is not trying to directly measure private mental models. It is not judging whether practitioners understand systems "correctly" in the abstract. It is asking how usable, situated understanding is constructed and maintained under partial observability.

## Relation to other RQs

- RQ1 `supports` RQ2 by showing how boundaries are interpreted in practice.
- RQ1 `supports` RQ3 by showing how safety/security concerns become visible or actionable.
- RQ1 `supports` RQ4 by showing how assurance-relevant understanding is maintained over change.
